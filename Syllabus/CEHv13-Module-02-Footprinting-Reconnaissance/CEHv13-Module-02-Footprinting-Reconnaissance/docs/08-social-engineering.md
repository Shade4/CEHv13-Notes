# 08 — Footprinting Through Social Engineering

Social engineering gathers information by influencing people rather than only querying technical systems.

## Why it works

Humans may:

- Trust authority
- Want to be helpful
- Fear consequences
- Respond to urgency
- Overshare
- Fail to verify identity

## Major techniques

### Eavesdropping

Obtaining information by listening to or intercepting communications without authorization.

Defensive controls:

- Use secure communication channels
- Avoid discussing sensitive data in public
- Train staff
- Use encryption where appropriate

### Shoulder surfing

Observing:

- Password entry
- Screen contents
- Sensitive documents
- Access codes

Defenses:

- Privacy screens
- Secure workspace design
- Awareness training
- Screen-lock policies

### Dumpster diving

Searching discarded materials for information.

Potential findings:

- Organizational charts
- Contact information
- Printed credentials
- System details
- Project information

Defenses:

- Secure shredding
- Media disposal procedures
- Document classification
- Disposal policies

### Impersonation

Pretending to be another person to obtain information or access.

Defenses:

- Identity verification
- Callback procedures
- Least privilege
- Security awareness
- Multi-factor authentication

## Ethical social-engineering testing

A professional engagement should define:

- Approved pretexts
- Approved targets
- Allowed channels
- Time windows
- Data that may be collected
- Stop conditions
- Emergency contacts
- Evidence handling
- Privacy protections

## Reconnaissance value

Social engineering can reveal:

- Technology names
- Organizational structure
- Internal processes
- Vendors
- Contact information
- Security procedures

However, social engineering is highly sensitive. It should never be treated as a casual experiment against real people.

## Defensive checklist

- Train employees
- Verify unusual requests
- Establish out-of-band verification
- Use MFA
- Protect employee information
- Sanitize public disclosures
- Disable former employee accounts
- Maintain clear reporting channels
- Conduct authorized simulations
