# Lab 01 — Passive OSINT

## Objective

Practice collecting public information without directly probing target infrastructure.

## Safe target

Use a domain you own or a training domain explicitly provided by your course.

## Tasks

- [ ] Record the domain.
- [ ] Identify official pages.
- [ ] Search for public PDF documents.
- [ ] Identify public technology references.
- [ ] Identify public employee/role information only when necessary.
- [ ] Record sources and timestamps.
- [ ] Separate facts from assumptions.

## Suggested searches

```text
site:example.com
site:example.com filetype:pdf
site:example.com intitle:"annual report"
site:example.com inurl:docs
```
