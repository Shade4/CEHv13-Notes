# Cybersecurity Glossary — Module 01

## A

### Active attack
An attack that interacts with, modifies, disrupts, or attempts to change the target environment.

### Advanced Persistent Threat (APT)
A long-running, often highly capable threat campaign characterized by persistence, adaptation, and targeted objectives.

### Availability
The property that authorized users can access systems and information when required.

---

## C

### CIA triad
Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability.

### Command and Control (C2)
A communication mechanism through which a compromised system communicates with attacker-controlled infrastructure.

### Confidentiality
Protection against unauthorized disclosure of information.

### Cyber Kill Chain
A seven-stage model used to understand and defend against cyber intrusion campaigns.

---

## D

### Defense in depth
A security strategy using multiple layers of controls.

### Distribution attack
An attack involving compromise or tampering during the distribution/supply-chain process.

---

## E

### Ethical hacking
Authorized security testing performed to identify and help remediate vulnerabilities.

### Exploitation
The act of taking advantage of a vulnerability or weakness.

---

## I

### Information warfare
Use of information and communication technologies to obtain strategic or competitive advantage over an opponent.

### Integrity
Protection of information from unauthorized modification and preservation of trustworthiness.

### Insider threat
Risk created when a trusted person misuses legitimate access or causes harm through negligence.

---

## N

### Non-repudiation
Evidence that helps prevent a party from credibly denying an attributable action.

### Reconnaissance
Information gathering about a target.

---

## P

### Passive attack
An attack focused on observing or intercepting information without directly changing the target.

### Penetration testing
An authorized security assessment in which testers attempt to demonstrate exploitable weaknesses under defined rules.

### Privilege escalation
Obtaining higher privileges than originally granted.

---

## T

### Tactic
A broad strategy or objective used by a threat actor.

### Technique
A technical method used to accomplish an intermediate objective.

### Threat
A potential source of harm or an event capable of exploiting a weakness.

### Threat actor
A person, group, organization, or other entity capable of conducting a security threat.

### TTP
Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures.

---

## V

### Vulnerability
A weakness that can be abused to compromise a security property or control.

### Vulnerability assessment
The process of identifying, analyzing, and classifying security weaknesses.

---

## W

### Weaponization
The stage in the Cyber Kill Chain where an attacker prepares a malicious payload or exploit for delivery.

### White-hat hacker
An authorized security professional who uses attacker-style techniques for defensive purposes.
